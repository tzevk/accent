# Proposal pricing uses reviewed cost assumptions, not live payroll rates

The Bid Desk design targets underpricing prevention for fixed-price Proposals. Delivery staff review estimated hours by discipline; named Employees are optional. Finance-approved Discipline Cost Rates price that effort, rather than live payroll averages. Salary changes must not silently change reviewed pricing. This does not change payroll's Hourly Rate under ADR-0010 or recorded Project Employee Cost under ADR-0016.

A Proposal Cost Estimate includes labour, direct expenses, supplier work, and a separate Company Overhead allowance calculated as a percentage of labour cost. Proposal Contingency is a separate reviewed amount with an explanation; zero requires explicit review. Foreign-currency Proposals require a finance-approved exchange-rate assumption saved with each pricing revision. Missing or unreviewed assumptions permit draft saving but block issuance; approval cannot turn an unknown cost into a supported price.

Sales staff without cost access receive price guidance, not underlying costs or exact margins. Authorized managers can inspect the cost basis. This prevents direct disclosure, not inference: a visible minimum price and a known margin rule can reveal approximate cost. Individual salaries are not exposed by the Bid Desk. Issuing a below-threshold Proposal requires Pricing Exception Approval with a recorded reason. The approver must be neither the Proposal creator nor an author of its current pricing revision. Permissions remain explicit, with no automatic grants.

Finance permissions control versioned discipline rates, overhead percentages, required margins, and exchange-rate assumptions. There are no invented initial rates or percentages: finance must configure them before issuance. Proposal Pricing Margin is price less the Proposal Cost Estimate and reviewed Proposal Contingency, divided by price; output GST is excluded. It is not markup or realized profit.

Pricing assumptions require explicit validity dates. Expired assumptions block new issuance until reviewed pricing is renewed. New policy versions never silently rewrite earlier reviewed pricing.

The launch scope is complete fixed-price pricing, review, exception approval, controlled issuance, and a preserved Project handoff baseline. Capacity checks, actual-versus-bid delivery reporting, and historical learning are explicitly outside this release.

Reviews and approvals bind one exact Proposal Pricing Revision. Changes to scope, estimated hours, costs, price, currency, rates, or validity require renewed review and any applicable exception approval. Existing Proposals retain their history, but new issuance, reissuance, and new revisions require the new pricing process. No historical reviews or approvals are fabricated.

Cost items retain their original currencies. Approved conversion assumptions normalize costs into INR, then translate price guidance into the Proposal currency. Tax treatment requires finance confirmation before issuance: irrecoverable tax is cost, confirmed recoverable tax is excluded, and output GST is not margin. Unknown recoverability blocks issuance rather than silently choosing a treatment.

Complete assumptions, delivery review, and compliance with finance-approved pricing policy permit ordinary issuance. Finance does not approve every Proposal individually. Below-minimum pricing still requires independent Pricing Exception Approval.

These decisions favor controlled pricing assumptions and cost confidentiality over automatic reuse of payroll figures. Issuance enforcement, remaining revision lifecycle choices, and acceptance criteria remain subject to the design interview. This records agreed design boundaries, not implemented behavior.
