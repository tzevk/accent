# Two punch-span implementations coexist: direction-gated day times, direction-agnostic Time Present

The codebase derives a day's punch span twice and the two answers differ. That is deliberate. `src/lib/punch.ts` gates on direction — `applyInferredDirections` fills a blank direction by alternating in → out across an employee-day's punches, collapsing a retry tap inside `TAP_COLLAPSE_SECONDS = 120` onto its predecessor's direction, and `deriveDayTimes` takes the first `in` and the last `out`. It feeds the employee attendance grid (`src/app/api/attendance/route.js` → `buildPunchDays` → `deriveDayTimes`/`deriveOvertime`) and through it Payable OT (`src/lib/attendance-summary.ts`, ADR-0006). `src/lib/time-present.ts` measures the day's last punch minus its first, direction-agnostic and pooled across devices, and feeds the Attendance report (`src/app/reports/attendance-report/data-source.ts`). Employee-day bucketing is shared (`bucketPunchIndicesByEmployeeDay`, `punch.ts`) so the two cannot drift on how a day is assembled; they disagree deliberately on how the day is measured.

The defect that forced the split is real and measured. Alternating in/out across a day's punches infers the final punch as an entry rather than an exit on an **odd** punch count, so `deriveDayTimes` ends the day at the **second** punch: a day punched 09:02 / 12:10 / 18:41 reads **3.13 hours** (09:02 → 12:10) where the true span is **9.65 hours** (09:02 → 18:41), and 71 of 356 employee-days on the fixture set were wrong. Payable OT was **not** changed along with the fix: it is a pay figure and the alternation is its evidence base, so altering it would move money (ADR-0010) on the strength of an inference the devices never made. That is why two implementations stay.

The cross-midnight merge for Time Present, and the shape of what the report shows:

- A next-day Punch joins the day only when it is chronologically **after** that day's last Punch and within `MAX_MERGED_SPAN_HOURS = 12` of that day's **first** Punch.
- The comparison is **absolute elapsed time**, never minutes-of-day.
- A merged Punch is **consumed** — shifted off the following day — so no Punch is ever counted toward two days.
- A single Punch is uncomputable (`hours: null`, rendered as an em dash), never `0`: zero would read as "was there and left instantly".
- A candidate beyond 12 hours is **refused**; the day is flagged `mergeRefused` and stays null rather than inventing an implausible presence.

The report's roster and payroll's roster are also different on purpose. The report filters the **Employee record** — `isDelete = 0`, `status = 'active'`, `employee_type = 'Payroll'` (`selectPayrollRoster`, `src/lib/payroll-roster.ts`) — and an Employee with **no** `employee_salary_profile` row is still on it (`has_salary_profile` is accepted and deliberately ignored). Payroll filters the **Salary Profile's** `salary_type` — `salary_type = 'contract'` EXISTS for the contract stream, NOT EXISTS for the payroll stream (`src/app/api/payroll/slips/route.js`, `src/app/api/payroll/export-sheet/route.js`, `generateMonthlyPayroll` in `src/utils/payroll-calculator.js`). The two sets cannot be the same set: a `Payroll`-typed Employee with no profile is on the report's roster and in neither payroll stream. Accepted because the report's roster is the company's payroll _directory_, so someone who never punched still gets a row, while payroll's roster is the pay _agreements_ that exist. The report discloses rather than hides it — `ROSTER_FILTER_DESCRIPTION` prints the filter on screen and `ROSTER_MEMBERSHIP_NOTE` prints the membership rule — so an employee missing from a payroll report is explained instead of becoming a support question.

Considered: (a) make the two calculators agree, or fix the alternation for Payable OT too — rejected on both counts, they answer different questions and Payable OT is money (ADR-0010, ADR-0006); (b) a fixed-hour-of-day merge cutoff (only punches before some hour join the day) — rejected, it cannot express a 06:30 finish, which is why the design got it wrong once already; (c) compare **minutes-of-day** rather than absolute elapsed time — rejected, 08:00 then 09:00 next morning is 25 hours elapsed but reads as 1 hour on a clock face, passing a tail through a guard it should have failed, the second time this design got it wrong; (d) give every day a direction-free span everywhere — rejected, it would rewrite the grid's in/out columns and Payable OT off an inference, and the SmartOffice devices report no direction to infer from (`docs/explanations/SMARTOFFICE_ATTENDANCE_PIPELINE.md`); (e) filter the report's roster by Salary Profile to match payroll — rejected, it would silently drop every Employee whose profile row is missing, which is precisely the person a presence report exists to surface.

Consequences: a night-shift worker who also works that evening gets **both** shifts, each at its own length, and no span exceeds 12 hours — the trade is that a genuine day longer than 12 hours reads uncomputable, which the report states on screen rather than guessing; Time Present stays uncapped on a half day, so a day authored `HD` shows its measured span against the grid's 4 credited hours (ADR-0007's half-day intent, not a discrepancy to reconcile); `MAX_MERGED_SPAN_HOURS` is interpolated into the on-screen copy so the stated threshold cannot drift from the calculator; the glossary now separates Time Present from Logged Hours, Capacity, Payable Day and Payable OT (GLOSSARY.md), and Employee Type from Salary Type, because reading Time Present as Logged Hours would silently re-price payroll and reading Employee Type as the payroll stream would pick the wrong column. A future reader must not make the two calculators agree, must not reintroduce direction gating into Time Present, and must not cap Logged Hours per day (ADR-0010).

Two further rules are recorded with this design.

**Punch attribution is by the ingest-stamped `attendance_logs.employee_id`, not the device code.** `employee_code` is display metadata; the stamp happens once, at ingest, against the code mapping in force at that moment, so a re-enrolment changes future matches only and the already-stored rows keep the employee they were stamped with. Readers — the report above all — must not filter or re-derive attribution by code.

**The Attendance report's Punch fetch pads one day either side of the month for computation** (`[month start − 1 day, next month start + 1 day)`). A shift crossing a month boundary merges on the day it began, because the day that began it is in view and the continuation Punch is one padded day beyond the month edge. The padded days feed the merge/consumption walk and are never rendered; cells, month statistics, the device list and the shipped drill-down payload stay month-scoped. A consumed continuation Punch still counts in its own day's punch count, because that count is about the cell's date, not about which span claimed the Punch.
