# Petty-cash funding is one event; spending is one cost

Ticket #316 makes the petty-cash source of the company expenditure report real: cash-voucher funding, the mirrored credit in the petty-cash ledger, actual spending with its destination and approval, and the references that stop one purchase being counted twice. This record fixes the decisions that later slices (financial close, revisions, export) build on, so petty cash feeds the same module instead of growing a parallel ledger interpretation.

The petty-cash ledger (`petty_cash_expenses`) holds two kinds of row, split by `entry_kind`. A `funding` row is cash into the float: one cash voucher and its mirrored credit are one funding event. It carries a funding-event identity (`fund-<voucherId>`, unique), is linked in `financial_cost_links` as `role='funding'` (voucher) and `role='mirror'` (credit), and is never registered as a cost, never recognized, and never counted in operating expenditure. Repeating the mirroring — a voucher edit, a repair, a retried request — updates that one row; it never adds another funding event. A voucher cannot be deleted while it funds spending, and a voucher's total cannot drop below the spending drawn from it.

A `spend` row is actual spending and is a cost-bearing source of the same module: it carries a canonical `cost_uid` (`cost-<uuid>`), a Recognition Period with its basis, an approval state, tax treatment, evidence, and a deliberate Project / Company Overhead / Unallocated classification. It is recognized through the same versioned-command contract as direct expenses (`expected_version`, one `financial_cost_events` row per accepted command, recognized state frozen against register edits and soft deletes), with its own privilege pair (`petty_cash_expenses:update` / `:approve`). Missing linkage is disclosed, never invented: an unclassified spend stays unresolved and cannot be recognized, and the voucher's free-text `project_number` is never read as a Project reference.

A receipt already linked to another cost settles that cost. `petty_cash_expenses.linked_cost_uid` holds the target `cost_uid`; it is resolved through the shared source seam (`resolveCostReference`) at capture and at every command, and a link that does not resolve fails the command instead of creating a new cost. A linked receipt registers `role='settlement'` and no cost identity of its own, so the underlying cost is counted once and the spending is visible as cash movement rather than a second expense.

Funding, spending, settled spending, unconfirmed spending, remaining supported funding, and recognized cost are stated separately in the report (`petty_cash` on the reconciliation) and in the register. Cash figures follow the cash date; recognized cost follows the Recognition Period; funding never enters the company total, and remaining funding is never operating cost. Petty-cash recognized cost feeds the company groups and Project rows like any other cost source, through the same `buildReconciliation`.

`recognized_amount` stays NULL for a settlement (it recognizes no new cost), and a cancelled spend keeps its row, its recognized amount, and its journal — closing and revisions read that history rather than a mutable status. The petty-cash summary is per currency; a multi-currency month states no combined petty-cash total, matching the module's currency rule.

The source lives in `src/lib/company-expenditure/petty-cash.ts`; the public functions are exported from `index.ts` (`recordPettyCashSpend`, `executePettyCashCommand`, `ensureFundingMirror`, `fetchPettyCashSummary`, and the JSON boundary mappers). No screen or route reimplements a period, tax, or currency rule.
