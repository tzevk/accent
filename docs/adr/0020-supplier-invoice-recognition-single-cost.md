# Supplier invoice recognition: one liability, one cost

Ticket #311 makes supplier invoices real in the company expenditure report. A supplier invoice is captured in the existing `purchase_invoices` register with the same financial identity and recognition fields as a direct cost (#306), recognized into its received-work month, and reconciled through the same module. This record fixes the decisions later slices (#312 order consumption, #313 accruals, #319 currency, #322 close) build on.

One supplier liability is one cost. The invoice row carries `cost_uid`; payable follow-ups, receipt copies, and settlements reference that identity through `financial_cost_links` and never become another cost. `financial_cost_links` is the shared registry and reference table (contract: `C:/Files/OCDSE/Work/expenditure-source-contract.md`): one `role='cost'` row per cost-bearing row in any store, plus foreign rows with a role, a basis, and a review state. Only `review_state='confirmed'` links are authoritative. A text match between a payable's document number and an invoice is preserved as a reviewable candidate and is never applied on its own; a decision (document-backed confirm, or reject) is recorded on the link row. `payment_payables.cost_uid` migrates existing `purchase_invoice_id` references to the canonical identity; the register refuses ad-hoc link rewrites.

An invoice covering several service periods records `supplier_invoice_periods` slices: each slice states its received-work period and gross amount, the slices total the invoice gross exactly, and recognition is refused while they do not (`split_total_mismatch`, `split_tax_mismatch`). Each month counts only its own slice — the full invoice is never repeated per period, and the slice-level recognized amount is frozen at recognition. A single-period invoice uses its own service period fields; the invoice date is only a disclosed fallback, and payment never creates cost (a September service stays September cost when invoiced in October and paid in November).

The register's native `status` and the financial `recognition_state` remain separate, as in #306: only an explicit, authorized `recognize` command creates confirmed cost, and the register `PUT`/`DELETE` refuse recognized cost and versioned financial fields. Commands are the versioned path (`financial_version` + one `financial_cost_events` row per accepted command, `source_table='purchase_invoices'`) and accept the caller's transaction.

Withholding tax (TDS) is settlement information and never reduces incurred cost. Recoverable tax is excluded only with its evidence; otherwise the gross stays in cost and stays visible. Free-text PO numbers remain descriptive evidence until a reliable order reference exists (#312); standalone invoices without any purchase order recognize normally.

Authorization is layered on the existing privileges: `purchase_orders:update` for the source register and its commands, plus `other_expenses:approve` for recognize, reject, cancel, and link decisions. A reader with neither receives no sensitive values and no writes.
