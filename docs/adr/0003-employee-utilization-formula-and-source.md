# Employee utilization formula and source

Utilization answers under/overwork per employee per month. Decided: `utilization% = total_logged / net_capacity × 100` where `total_logged` is the uncapped sum of `user_activity_assignments.daily_entries.hours` (overtime included) and `net_capacity = working_days × 8 − leave_days × 8 (HD × 4)`, with working days excluding Sundays, 2nd/4th Saturdays (`isScheduledWeeklyOff`) and active `holiday_master` dates, reusing `timesheet-report` helpers (`statusKind`, `dayTypeFor`, `computeMonthlyHours` cap/overtime split) instead of parallel logic.

**Cost addendum (prioritization):** each row also carries `monthly_cost` (CTC basis: `employer_cost || gross_salary || gross` per `PayrollPage.summarizeProfile` — stored CTC first) and `bench_cost = monthly_cost − ctc_rate × logged_hours`, where `ctc_rate` mirrors `computeRawHourlyRate` but denominated in CTC (direct rate for hourly/daily/custom types, else monthly over `std_working_days` default 26 × `std_hours_per_day` default 8). Profile picked by `pickActiveProfile`; no covering profile → hours+% shown, cost blank with `no-profile` flag, never zero. No mid-month pro-rating (full-month capacity).

**Considered Options**: total-vs-gross capacity (rejected: approved leave would false-flag as underwork); capped-vs-uncapped numerator (rejected cap: overwork must show as >100%); TwentyCRM as reference (rejected: sales CRM, no resourcing concept — Accent's own timesheet/manhours pattern is the source, formula cross-checked against ERPNext Project Profitability); Gross-first cost (rejected: reports' `resolveMonthlySalary` convention prices billing rate, but bench decisions need true burn — CTC-first deliberately diverges, documented here); gross-rate reuse for bench (rejected: mixing CTC monthly with gross-based rate breaks `fractional + bench = monthly` footing); masked cost columns (rejected for v1: both cost reports already expose salary-derived rates under the same triple-OR `project_activities` grant — follow precedent, harden later).
**Consequences**: inherits and therefore must first fix the `leave-helpers.ts` `holiday_date`-vs-`date` bug; v1 is team table sorted by flag band then bench desc (biggest bleed first) with month + flag filters, at `/reports/employee-utilization` reusing the `project_activities` grant and the `page + data-source + route + download` Excel shape; CTC stored at profile-save time can go stale after payroll-rule changes — accepted, same staleness the payroll tiles already carry.

## Amendment (2026-10-05): utilization v2 — payroll roster, employment window, payroll-aligned rate, lenses

Utilization v2 (#292) supersedes three statements above: the roster is the **Payroll Roster**, not every active employee; Capacity and Monthly Cost are pro-rated to the employment window; and Bench Cost is priced with **CTC ÷ Basis Hours**, payroll's own rate, instead of the profile/direct-rate denominators. The core formula (`total_logged / net_capacity × 100`), the 80/100 bands and the totals' flag-filtered scope are unchanged (the no-log count alone is month-wide, matching the department rollup).

**Roster scope.** A viewed month is scoped to the shared payroll roster (`src/lib/payroll-roster.ts`): an Employee record with `isDelete = 0` and `employee_type = 'Payroll'` whose **employment window intersects the month**. `status` no longer disqualifies in month mode — a leaver stays visible in the months they worked, and a joiner is absent from months before they joined. Employee Type is read as of today (a retyped employee is not time-traveled — accepted limitation) and is the roster switch, never Salary Type: an Employee with no Salary Profile is still on the roster (ADR-0015). The payload carries the selector's exclusion disclosure — the non-Payroll/unset employees among the live employees whose window intersects the month, bucketed by Employee Type value (unset as its own bucket), absent when nothing was dropped — so a misfiled type is caught rather than silently hidden; an employee the window cannot place in any month is outside the month's scope entirely, not disclosed. The Attendance report keeps its as-of-today roster (`status = 'active'`), the split ADR-0015 documents.

**Employment window.** `resolveEmploymentWindow` resolves one window per employee:

- start = `joining_date` → `hire_date` → the earliest evidence day across `employee_attendance.attendance_date`, the Logged Hours days (`user_activity_assignments.daily_entries[].date`), `user_screen_time.date` and the `user_activity_logs.created_at` day → open when the status is active (unresolved otherwise);
- end = `exit_date` → the latest evidence day across the same four sources → open when the status is active (unresolved otherwise).

`null` is an open bound (unbounded on that side); an unresolved bound places the employee in no month. The evidence bounds are the **min/max across attendance, Logged Hours, screen time and activity logs** — the earliest/latest day any of them records, never a source-priority chain that lets an older attendance record cap a newer Logged Hours day; equal days keep source order (attendance, Logged Hours, screen time, activity logs). Screen time and activity logs are keyed by user and mapped to the Employee through `users.employee_id`. A month is in scope when the window overlaps it.

**Pro-rating.** Capacity counts only the working days inside the window (the weekly-off and leave rules inside it are unchanged; days outside it land in no bucket). Monthly Cost = `monthly × employed working days ÷ month working days` on the capacity calendar, 2dp via `src/lib/money.ts`; a full-month window reproduces the full CTC, and a window with no working days costs 0. Rows whose window does not cover the whole month carry `is_partial_window` and the page names the clamped dates ("Partial (15 Jan – 31 Jan)"). The utilized figure (Hourly Rate × logged hours) plus Bench Cost still foots to Monthly Cost per row and in totals.

**Rate alignment (closes ADR-0010's follow-up).** The bench/utilized rate is CTC ÷ Basis Hours, where Basis Hours = the month's basis days (every non-Sunday day minus the active non-optional holidays; 2nd/4th Saturdays stay in) × the profile's `std_hours_per_day` (default 8) — payroll's `getWorkingDaysForMonth` rule. Direct stored hourly/daily/custom rates and `std_working_days` no longer price the report, and a month a profiled employee fully logs reconciles with its Payroll Slip. The rate calendar and the Capacity calendar differ by design and the report states so on screen. This supersedes the Cost addendum's `ctc_rate` definition and closes ADR-0010's follow-up note that the utilization report's Bench Cost keeps its own 26 × 8 CTC rate and the two rates diverge by month.

**Optional holidays.** Only active non-optional holidays subtract from Capacity; an active optional holiday is a full working day, matching the attendance and payroll paths. The `is_optional` flag is the switch — `holiday_master.type` is ignored by every consumer.

**New lenses.** The grid gains: a `no_time_logged` state and count for zero-Logged-Hours rows (they keep their Under band and band-then-bench slot); trailing Utilization for the viewed month and the two before it, blank where the window does not cover the month, with a "chronic under" marker when every employed month of the window reads below 80 with a real percent (at least two employed months); a six-month capacity-weighted trend series with the priced rows' Bench Cost; a Department column, filter and summary table (unset grouped as "Unassigned"; the rollup describes the month, computed before the band filter); and a per-employee project breakdown — top five projects, "Other" and an explicit "No project" bucket, linking to the month's timesheet. The Excel export mirrors the columns, the disclosure and the summary.

**Preserved semantics.** The 80/100 thresholds; band-then-Bench-Cost ordering; the month, band and search filters; totals computed over the flag-filtered row set — save the no-log count, which counts the viewed month like the department rollup; blank (never zero) costs without a covering profile; client-side search and department filters that narrow only the rendered rows; and the unchanged access gate.
