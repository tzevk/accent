# Project Margin & Scope Radar

**Status:** Proposed. Not implemented. **Business value rank:** 1 of 4.

## Business case

Recover fees for extra work and catch overruns before the team commits more time. The director needs decisions, not another project status report.

## Proposed workflow

1. A Project Lead flags work outside the agreed scope. They link the scope baseline and evidence, such as a client request or meeting note.
2. The lead records what changed, work already done, remaining effort, estimated cost, and a decision deadline. Unknown costs stay marked as unknown.
3. Finance validates material figures and confirms which work is already covered by an approved change. The original scope and budget remain visible.
4. The weekly **Director’s Decision Brief** shows up to five open items. Each shows the money at risk, the evidence, the owner, and the decision needed. Prioritize deadline and financial exposure. Keep unpriced items visible when delay would be costly.
5. The director records a decision: request a change order, pause work, or absorb the cost. The lead records the outcome and links any approved client change.

## Cost rules and boundaries

- Use the approved Project budget as the comparison point. Keep original and approved revised budgets distinct.
- Forecast cost at completion from incurred cost plus remaining costs, including open supplier commitments only once. Do not count work already recognized through an invoice or accrual again.
- Keep payroll-based estimates separate from recorded cost. Do not treat missing timesheets or cost records as zero.
- Track requested change value separately from approved value. A director decision does not amend the budget or send anything to the client.
- Enforce existing Project access and explicit cost permissions. Do not expose individual pay in the brief.

## Pilot and success measures

Pilot on five active Projects. Track approved change-order value, time from flag to decision, and decisions made before work continues. Report approved value separately from cash collected.

Before rollout, confirm who validates cost, what warrants escalation, and who can authorize a pause. Verify that a purchase order partly consumed by an accrual contributes only its remaining commitment.
