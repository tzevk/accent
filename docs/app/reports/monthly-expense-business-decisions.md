# Monthly expense business decisions — research note

**Status:** management-reporting research note, 2026-10-16. Not an accounting-policy decision (ADR/glossary owned elsewhere) and not a certification of statutory compliance. It records what the report at `src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/` does today, where it falls short for monthly-expense decisions, and which improvements to build first. No application code, migration, test, or existing doc was changed for this note.

**How sources are cited:** every factual claim below names the file and lines that own it. `docs/explanations/notes.md` is explicitly "not a verified list of open work" (`docs/explanations/notes.md:1-8`), so scratch lines there are cited only as request traces, never as proof of behavior. The repo's published report guides live in `docs/app/reports/` (`docs/README.md:8-16`), which is why this note lives beside them; the docs index has not been updated because this task owns only this one new file (`docs/README.md:79-85` describes index maintenance as a separate step).

**Question asked:** "find out monthly expenses" — the client wants a way to see what a month cost, whether that figure is firm or provisional, what drove it, and what it implies for spending control. The accepted spec phrases the same need as monthly Company Incurred Cost, separated Project/Overhead/Unallocated cost, current-month default, estimate-vs-recorded certainty, rankings, and separate commitments/cash (`docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-spec.md:23-38`).

## 1. What the report does today

### 1.1 Three views, one default

- The page offers three view tabs — Expenditure, Monthly, Financial Year — and opens on `expenditure` (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/page.tsx:509`, `src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/page.tsx:1548-1585`).
- The Expenditure view is the company reconciliation view; the Monthly and FY views are employee-cost views that sit alongside it (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/page.tsx:1540-1546`, `src/app/api/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/route.ts:1-17`).
- Readers without both `other_expenses:read` and `payroll:read` are not offered Expenditure and fall back to the Monthly employee-cost view; the server refuses the expenditure endpoints either way (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/page.tsx:620-640`, `src/app/api/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/route.ts:113-122`).
- The Expenditure picker lists months carrying direct cost plus the current selection; the employee-cost picker lists months derived from timesheet dates plus the current month (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/page.tsx:655-663`, `src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/data-source.ts:1059-1104`).

### 1.2 Expenditure view: the reconciled monthly expense

- The view "leads with Company Incurred Cost and reconciles it to Incurred Project Cost, Company Overhead, and Unallocated Cost, each direct cost counted once" (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/expenditure-view.tsx:3-11`).
- The four headline KPIs are Company Incurred Cost, Incurred Project Cost, Company Overhead, and Unallocated Cost (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/expenditure-view.tsx:1315-1400`).
- Company Incurred Cost is operating cost recognized in a period, whether paid or unpaid, and "includes direct Project costs, Company Overhead, and Unallocated Cost, each counted once" (`GLOSSARY.md:95-97`); Incurred Project Cost is "employee cost and non-employee expenses recognized for a Project in a period, whether paid or unpaid" and "excludes unfulfilled supplier commitments and client order value" (`GLOSSARY.md:143-145`).
- The shared module guarantees confirmed cost is `recognition_state = 'recognized'` and nothing else, a missing amount is NULL never zero, the Recognition Period comes from the received-work period (bill date only as disclosed fallback), and currencies are never added without supported conversion (`src/lib/company-expenditure/index.ts:61-77`).
- Recognition Period "comes from the received-work/service period, or from the bill date as a disclosed fallback" and "an order date or a payment date never sets it" (`GLOSSARY.md:123-125`); Recognition State is `draft`/`pending_evidence`/`recognized`/`rejected`/`cancelled` and "only `recognized` is confirmed cost" (`GLOSSARY.md:127-129`).
- The comparable-period panel measures the reported month against its prior period, stating current cost, prior cost, absolute change, and percentage change with its change state (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/expenditure-view.tsx:1402-1499`).
- A percentage is stated only when supported: a known-zero prior is "New cost, no percentage" and an unknown prior stays unknown (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/expenditure-view.tsx:629-639`).
- The comparison rules are: unfinished months compare equivalent elapsed service periods never against a full prior month; zero prior shows absolute change and new-cost state with no invented percentage; late/backdated/day-less entries are disclosed; a Project with no prior-window cost has unknown prior, not zero (`src/lib/company-expenditure/ranking.ts:1-20`).
- A Comparable Period for an unfinished month is the first N days of both months where N is the `as_of` day (today by default), with clamping disclosed; a cost is inside only when its received-work period is fully dated and wholly inside the window (`GLOSSARY.md:147-149`).
- Project rows carry current cost, prior comparison cost, change amount/percent/state, cost-to-date, late-entry counts, evidence state, employee cost, estimated employee cost, logged hours, and employee count (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/expenditure-view.tsx:120-145`).
- Cost to Date is "cumulative confirmed cost of every month before the reported one plus the reported window" and "a contributing unknown amount makes it unknown (`null`), not zero" (`GLOSSARY.md:151-153`).
- The two Project Cost Ranking orderings rank "the same Project rows inside one currency: largest monthly incurred cost, and largest change against the Comparable Period", with unknown-comparison rows reported unranked with reasons and ties sharing positions (`GLOSSARY.md:155-157`); the view applies the payload's ordering rather than re-sorting locally (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/expenditure-view.tsx:1037-1070`).
- Financial-year stepping moves the same month one year at a time, never past the current month, and an empty target month reports coverage rather than an invented zero (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/expenditure-view.tsx:1072-1097`).
- Coverage notices render as a list with code and severity (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/expenditure-view.tsx:1916-1923`); all seven declared sources are currently `wired`, including direct expenses, other expenses, recorded payroll, supplier invoices, accruals, cash paid, and petty cash (`src/lib/company-expenditure/coverage.ts:23-73`).

### 1.3 Employee-cost interpretation behind every view

- Employee cost follows ADR-0016: "allocate each Employee's recorded Payroll Slip employer cost, including earnings and employer contributions, by each Project's share of that Employee's monthly Logged Hours" and "show estimates separately before payroll finalization" (`docs/adr/0016-project-employee-cost-from-recorded-payroll.md:3`).
- The monthly/FY data-source header states the same rule: "recorded employer-cost allocations frozen at Payroll Finalize where they exist, the corrected payroll calculation's estimate otherwise" and the obsolete gross-first billing-derived rate path "is no longer used by these views" (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/data-source.ts:17-24`).
- Hours are `user_activity_assignments.daily_entries` timesheet logs bucketed into calendar months (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/data-source.ts:17-19`); Logged Hours are the sum of `daily_entries.hours` and the payroll numerator (`GLOSSARY.md:83-85`).
- How a row's cost is known is a four-state status: `recorded` | `estimated` | `known_zero` | `unknown` (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/data-source.ts:644-649`); the module defines the same four states, where `known_zero` is a finalized slip whose recorded employer cost is zero and `unknown` means no Salary Profile covers the month (`src/lib/company-expenditure/types.ts:1096-1105`).
- The month loader derives status as recorded (or known-zero when the frozen amount is zero), else estimated when a payroll-based estimate exists, else unknown (`src/lib/company-expenditure/payroll.ts:829-835`).
- Recorded cost applies only when a slip and a frozen allocation exist and the run is locked (`finalized`/`paid`) (`src/lib/company-expenditure/payroll.ts:675-678`, `src/lib/company-expenditure/payroll.ts:758-759`).
- The estimate path calls the payroll calculation with the month's Salary Profile, effective Component Rates, working days, and logged hours, and treats failure as unknown (`src/lib/company-expenditure/payroll.ts:770-791`); the Component Rates loader reads `payroll_schedules` for the priced date (`src/utils/payroll-calculator.js:41-60`).
- A historical bonus double-count (bonus raising both earnings and employer cost) was observed at runtime and documented as an estimate-composition risk affecting new calculations, not the five stored zero-bonus slips (`docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-design.md:123-127`, `docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-accounting.md:82`); the current calculation excludes bonus from employer contributions with a comment citing that issue and computes employer cost as earnings plus employer-only contributions (`src/utils/payroll-calculation.js:745-759`), while earnings still include bonus (`src/utils/payroll-calculation.js:513-524`).
- Allocation is deterministic cents: exact shares floored to the cent, leftover cents to largest fractional remainders with canonical tie-breaks, summing exactly to the slip (`src/lib/company-expenditure/payroll.ts:101-140`); the migration persists shares plus the rounding adjustment and snapshots employee/project identity (`migrations/20261008090700_payroll_employee_cost_allocation.js:1-39`).
- Hours without a Project stay in the denominator and their share is Unallocated Employee Cost ("No project"); with no Logged Hours the whole recorded cost stays unallocated ("No logged hours"), never redistributed (`src/lib/company-expenditure/payroll.ts:1-22`, `migrations/20261008090700_payroll_employee_cost_allocation.js:4-9`).
- The monthly builder maps a null-project share to `NO-PROJECT` ("No project") or `NO-LOGGED-HOURS` ("No logged hours") by its basis (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/data-source.ts:658-695`).
- Unallocated Employee Cost is "recorded payroll employer cost that cannot be attributed to a Project" and "is not Bench Cost" (`GLOSSARY.md:206-208`); Unallocated Cost generally is "recognized cost awaiting reliable classification" and includes both employee and non-employee unclassified cost (`GLOSSARY.md:103-105`).
- Both payroll and contract pay streams are included regardless of Employee Type, and the Utilization report's narrower Payroll Roster is not the cost-report boundary (`docs/adr/0016-project-employee-cost-from-recorded-payroll.md:12`, `docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-spec.md:101`); the frozen allocation stores `pay_stream` at freeze time (`migrations/20261008090700_payroll_employee_cost_allocation.js:51`).
- The month's employee-cost position states recorded total, estimated total, allocated total, unallocated total (No project + No logged hours), hour splits, rounding adjustment, and counts of recorded/known-zero/estimated/missing-slip/missing-pricing/allocation-missing (`src/lib/company-expenditure/types.ts:1233-1253`).
- One Employee's monthly position additionally carries logged/project/no-project hours, `no_logged_hours`, the three missing-data flags, the slip/allocation source pointer with kind (`finalization`/`reconstruction`/`revision`), money shares, all hours by project independent of pricing, and the latest reconstruction summary (`src/lib/company-expenditure/types.ts:1192-1231`).
- A recorded Employee's displayed hours are the frozen allocation's hours, so later timesheet edits cannot move them (`src/lib/company-expenditure/payroll.ts:815-827`); the freeze is written inside Payroll Finalize's transaction with the run lock (`src/lib/company-expenditure/payroll.ts:18-22`).
- Later timesheet changes must not silently change finalized costs; corrections travel through an explicit allocation revision preserving prior figures and reasons (`docs/adr/0016-project-employee-cost-from-recorded-payroll.md:9`); a Project Cost Allocation Revision "does not itself change the employee's payroll cost" (`GLOSSARY.md:210-212`).
- Finalized months without saved allocations are reconstructed once from recorded cost plus available timesheets, marked reconstructed with date, reviewed before freezing, and never presented as original finalization-time attribution (`docs/adr/0016-project-employee-cost-from-recorded-payroll.md:15`); a reconstruction is stored as a proposal until approved (`GLOSSARY.md:214-216`).
- Payroll coverage codes disclose: run not generated, run not finalized, slips missing, pricing missing (unknown, not zero), allocations missing (including the finalized-but-no-allocations case), approved reconstructions, pending reconstructions, recorded cost without hours, hours without project, and revised allocations (`src/lib/company-expenditure/payroll.ts:953-1062`).
- The Expenditure view's payroll summary states Recorded, Estimated, and Unallocated (No project + No logged hours) with hour splits (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/expenditure-view.tsx:2977-3015`).

### 1.4 Monthly view: single-month employee cost

- The monthly payload carries month labels, detailed employee×project rows, per-employee rows, per-project rows, totals (hours, cost, recorded, estimated, blended rate, counts), plus the full `payroll` position and `coverage` array (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/data-source.ts:217-261`).
- Each monthly row carries hours, cost, `cost_status`, and separate `recorded_cost`/`estimated_cost` (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/data-source.ts:195-215`); the builder sets `recorded_cost` to the share when recorded else zero, and `estimated_cost` to the complementary portion (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/data-source.ts:701-747`).
- Monthly totals sum hours/cost/recorded/estimated with Decimal math and count distinct employees and real projects (null-project buckets excluded from the project count) (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/data-source.ts:854-885`).
- The fetcher validates `YYYY-MM`, loads the employee index plus the month interpretation, builds all three row sets and totals, and derives the FY label (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/data-source.ts:1280-1315`); the route serves it at `?view=monthly&month=YYYY-MM` (`src/app/api/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/route.ts:291-312`).
- On screen, Monthly offers three breakdown tabs: Project Wise detail, By Employee, Project Summary (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/page.tsx:1684-1708`).
- Project Wise renders one printable section per project with project/client/month headers and employee rows (hourly cost, working hours, amount) plus per-project and all-project totals (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/page.tsx:270-415`).
- By Employee and By Project render sortable-free tables with grand-total footers (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/page.tsx:912-1101`).
- The five summary cards are Total Hours, Avg Rate/Hr (blended), Total Cost for the month, Employees, Projects (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/page.tsx:1728-1791`).
- Rows sort alphabetically — detailed and FY rows by employee name then project code; project aggregates by project code then name (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/data-source.ts:738-745`, `src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/data-source.ts:843-850`).
- The page's own `MonthlyRow`/`MonthlyEmployeeRow`/`MonthlyProjectRow`/`MonthlyCompanyCostData` interfaces carry only hours/cost/rate/counts and omit `cost_status`, `recorded_cost`, `estimated_cost`, `payroll`, and `coverage` (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/page.tsx:43-98`); a search of the page finds no rendering of those confidence fields, only the footnote below (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/page.tsx:1858-1867` grep evidence).
- The Monthly/FY footer footnote says "cost = payroll hourly rate in force that month × hours logged that month (hourly/daily rate when set, else gross salary ÷ standard working days × hours per day)" and that "these employee-cost views are estimates until a month's payroll is finalized" (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/page.tsx:1857-1867`).
- That footnote formula no longer matches the data-source header, which states the obsolete gross-first billing-derived rate path "is no longer used by these views" (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/data-source.ts:20-24`).
- Display hourly rate is derived cost ÷ hours with Decimal `ROUND_HALF_UP` to 2dp, zero when hours are zero (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/data-source.ts:288-295`); money uses global Decimal precision 20 and `ROUND_HALF_UP` (`src/lib/money.ts:1-11`).
- Search filters rows client-side across employee/project/client/department fields (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/page.tsx:708-730`); the cards and grand totals still read the unfiltered month totals (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/page.tsx:815-816`, `src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/page.tsx:753-783`).

### 1.5 FY view: twelve-month employee-cost matrix

- The FY payload carries FY labels, Apr–Mar month names/keys, detailed rows, employee aggregates, project aggregates, monthly totals, and summary counts (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/data-source.ts:177-193`).
- Each FY allocation row carries per-month hours/cost maps plus FY totals and separate FY `recorded_cost`/`estimated_cost` (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/data-source.ts:887-907`); the builder accumulates monthly and FY figures and splits each share into recorded vs estimated by whether the month had a recorded amount (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/data-source.ts:913-986`).
- FY totals accumulate monthly hours/cost with Decimal math plus FY recorded/estimated splits and a blended rate (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/data-source.ts:988-1027`).
- The fetcher loads all twelve FY calendar months (April first) and aggregates them (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/data-source.ts:638-642`, `src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/data-source.ts:1107-1152`); the route serves it at `?view=fy&fy=YYYY` (`src/app/api/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/route.ts:314-329`).
- On screen the FY view shows one metric at a time — Hours or Cost — via a toggle (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/page.tsx:1624-1648`); each cell renders the selected metric and the footer renders the matching monthly totals (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/page.tsx:1198-1257`).
- The FY summary cards mirror Monthly at FY grain: FY Total Hours, Avg Rate/Hr, FY Total Cost, Employees, Projects (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/page.tsx:1792-1853`).
- FY rows drop zero rows only when both total hours and total cost are zero (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/data-source.ts:973-975`); per-month recorded/estimated splits exist only as FY totals, not per cell (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/data-source.ts:899-904`).

### 1.6 Budget, cash, close, and revision sections

- The budget section compares approved Project cost budgets with Incurred Project Cost and states that "remaining budget is not profit, recognized revenue, or a forecast" (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/budget-section.tsx:3-14`).
- A variance publishes only when an approved budget matches Project, currency, scope (`project_incurred_cost`), and period exactly equal to the selected month; annual/partial budgets are disclosed as incompatible period and never allocated proportionally (`src/lib/company-expenditure/budget-comparison.ts:1-25`).
- Missing, unapproved, incompatible, or ambiguous budgets are stated explicitly; no commercial field, quotation, or order value is ever read as a budget (`src/lib/company-expenditure/budget-comparison.ts:11-12`); the outcomes vocabulary is compared/missing/unapproved/incompatible-currency/scope/period/ambiguous/unsupported/no-cost (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/budget-section.tsx:128-138`).
- The month's cost must be supported by a confirmed cost-bearing item; an unconfirmed month is never treated as supported zero (`src/lib/company-expenditure/budget-comparison.ts:13-17`).
- Budget scope distinguishes `project_incurred_cost` (comparable) from `commercial_value` (context only) (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/budget-section.tsx:148-151`, `migrations/20261008092100_project_cost_budgets.js:21-24`); budgets live in `project_cost_budgets` with amount, currency, period, state, approval evidence, and versioned journal (`migrations/20261008092100_project_cost_budgets.js:47-75`).
- The cash section renders dated outward cash — recorded settlements, native payroll payouts, dated petty-cash spending — with bank-into-float funding as its own movement set outside paid, per-target partial/final/unsettled cover, and legacy gaps disclosed never counted (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/cash-section.tsx:3-13`).
- Dated outward cash paid is the month's recorded settlements plus native payroll payouts (`payment_status='paid'` with real `payment_date`) plus dated petty-cash spending, each counted once by canonical movement identity; manual settlements never restate native movements (`docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-spec.md:126`, `src/lib/company-expenditure/cash.ts:1-25`).
- Outward Cash Paid counts `settle:<uid>`, `payroll:<slipId>`, `petty:<rowId>` once each; funding is `fund:<voucherId>` shown outside paid; receipts/transfers/undated/unlinked rows are disclosed legacy evidence never counted (`GLOSSARY.md:163-165`).
- The cash section starts collapsed (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/cash-section.tsx:143`) and its payload carries paid totals by currency, funding, targets with liability/settled/remaining/state, coverage counts, unresolved targets, and legacy buckets (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/cash-section.tsx:59-94`).
- Recording a settlement changes no incurred cost, commitment, or project row (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/cash-section.tsx:10-13`); supplier commitments and cash paid are separate from incurred cost by decision (`docs/adr/0017-project-incurred-cost-and-commitments-are-separate.md:3-7`).
- Financial Close saves a reviewed month with immutable figures; unresolved exceptions, unwired sources, unsupported commitments, and empty months block close, while pending payroll/partial cash/legacy gaps are warnings; once closed, ordinary writes are refused with `409 month_closed` and only explicit revisions change figures (`GLOSSARY.md:266-268`, `src/lib/company-expenditure/close.ts:135-176`).
- The close snapshot is one row per closed month holding the full `CompanyReconciliation` JSON plus review actor/timestamp/reason/evidence (`migrations/20261008092200_expenditure_financial_close.ts:3-20`, `migrations/20261008092200_expenditure_financial_close.ts:47-68`).
- The close section renders open (review blockers/warnings) vs closed (frozen totals + closure review) and offers the close command to authorized operators (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/close-section.tsx:3-19`, `src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/close-section.tsx:86-242`); it starts expanded (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/close-section.tsx:50`).
- The revision section renders open-month vs closed-month revision state and offers reasoned, evidenced corrections/reversals of closed figures with linked order consumptions following in the same transaction (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/revision-section.tsx:3-19`); a Financial Revision preserves prior/new figures and never rewrites the frozen close (`GLOSSARY.md:270-272`).
- Close and revision payloads ride the expenditure JSON response so the sections never fire second per-mount requests (`src/app/api/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/route.ts:195-223`); the Monthly/FY branches return no close/revision payload (`src/app/api/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/route.ts:291-329`).

### 1.8 Money, formatting, and schema anchors

- All money uses Decimal with `ROUND_HALF_UP`; numbers leave the module only at the JSON boundary (`src/lib/money.ts:1-11`, `src/lib/money.ts:49-52`, `src/lib/company-expenditure/cash.ts:17-21`).
- The INR formatter is the default for employee-cost screens/exports (`src/lib/format.js:35-40`), while the reconciliation must use per-currency formatting and never label unknown-currency money as INR (`src/lib/format.js:49-75`, `src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/expenditure-view.tsx:664-673`).
- Payroll is recorded in INR (`src/lib/company-expenditure/payroll.ts:51`).
- Each Payroll Slip stores its hours basis (`ctc_used`, `basis_hours`, `hourly_rate`, `logged_hours`) so the snapshot stays auditable after profiles/timesheets change (`migrations/20260925120000_add_slip_hours_basis_columns.js:1-20`).
- Salary Profile resolution and CTC apportionment over Basis Hours live in the manhours-billing data source the cost report imports (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/data-source.ts:30-40`); CTC is monthly pay base apportioned over Basis Hours to give Hourly Rate (`GLOSSARY.md:53-68`).

## 2. Ranked improvements for monthly-expense decisions

Ranked by business-decision value: what lets the owner answer "what did this month cost, can I trust it, what drove it, and what should I do?" with the least risk of acting on a wrong number. Guardrails in §4 apply to every item.

| Rank | Improvement                                                                 | Decision it unlocks                                       | Why it ranks here                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ---: | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|    1 | Surface recorded / estimated / unknown + coverage in Monthly and FY views   | "Is this month's expense firm or provisional?"            | The server already computes the split and the page drops it (§1.4); acting on an estimate as final is the costliest mistake this report can cause. ### R1 — Confidence in Monthly/FY: recorded / estimated / unknown + coverage

- **Problem.** The monthly payload's confidence fields (`cost_status`, `recorded_cost`, `estimated_cost`, `payroll`, `coverage`) are computed and returned (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/data-source.ts:195-261`, `src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/data-source.ts:1280-1315`) but the Monthly/FY screens render only combined cost (§1.4). The only caution is the footnote's blanket "estimates until finalized" (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/page.tsx:1857-1867`), which cannot distinguish a month that is 95% recorded from one that is 5% recorded.
- **Evidence already available.** Per-row status and splits (§1.4), month position totals and missing-data counts (`src/lib/company-expenditure/types.ts:1233-1253`), eleven payroll coverage codes (`src/lib/company-expenditure/payroll.ts:953-1062`), and per-Employee status labels already used in Expenditure (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/expenditure-view.tsx:653-659`).
- **Change.** Add to Monthly cards and table headers: Recorded, Estimated, Unknown-count, and % recorded; add a Status column (Recorded/Estimated/Known zero/Unknown) to detailed/by-employee rows; render the month's coverage notices above the table (reusing the expenditure coverage list pattern at `src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/expenditure-view.tsx:1916-1923`); in FY, add a per-month recorded/estimated strip under the existing monthly totals and FY-level recorded/estimated cards (the FY totals already split recorded/estimated at `src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/data-source.ts:1018-1023`).
- **Must not.** Never sum recorded and estimated into a single "total" without showing the split (ADR-0016 requires estimates shown separately at `docs/adr/0016-project-employee-cost-from-recorded-payroll.md:3`); never render unknown as zero (spec at `docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-spec.md:103`, module invariant at `src/lib/company-expenditure/index.ts:61-67`).

### R2 — Month totals, trend, and variance in Monthly/FY

- **Problem.** Monthly is one month with no prior-month change; FY shows twelve monthly totals in the footer (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/page.tsx:1233-1250`) but no change amount, percent, or trend, and forces Hours-or-Cost viewing (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/page.tsx:1624-1648`). Only Expenditure has the comparable-period panel and rankings (§1.2), which fallback Monthly readers never see (§1.1).
- **Evidence already available.** The comparison discipline (elapsed-equivalence, new-cost-no-percentage, unknown-stays-unknown) is implemented and tested as pure functions (`src/lib/company-expenditure/ranking.ts:1-20`); FY monthly Cost already exists server-side (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/data-source.ts:988-1027`).
- **Change.** In Monthly: add prior-month cost, absolute change, percent-or-state (reusing `percentLabel` semantics at `src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/expenditure-view.tsx:629-639`), and a 12-month cost strip (recorded vs estimated stacked) above the table. In FY: add a variance row under the monthly totals (MoM change per month), a YTD average column, and an option to show Hours and Cost side by side instead of toggling. For unfinished months, apply the same elapsed-period rule Expenditure uses — never compare a partial month against a full prior month (`src/lib/company-expenditure/ranking.ts:4-6`, `docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-spec.md:52-53`).
- **Must not.** Do not show a favorable percentage against a full previous month for an unfinished month (`docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-design.md:107`); do not invent a percentage for zero/unknown priors (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/expenditure-view.tsx:629-639`).

### R3 — Unallocated share as a monthly KPI

- **Problem.** `NO-PROJECT` and `NO-LOGGED-HOURS` rows exist in the data (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/data-source.ts:658-695`) and sort alphabetically into long tables, but Monthly/FY show no unallocated amount, share %, or trend. The owner cannot see leakage or timesheet-discipline drift. The design profile warns this slice can dominate: 2,922 of 2,930 September hours had no live project (`docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-design.md:31`), and that profile also notes most non-labour stores were empty so absence of rows is not absence of cost (`docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-design.md:17-35`).
- **Evidence already available.** Month `unallocated_total` and hour splits (`src/lib/company-expenditure/types.ts:1238-1244`), per-row destinations (§1.3), and the two unallocated coverage notices (`src/lib/company-expenditure/payroll.ts:1030-1047`).
- **Change.** Add Unallocated Cost and Unallocated % cards to Monthly/FY headers; add an attribution-rate column (% of each employee/project cost on real projects); add a "show unallocated only" filter; in FY, add an unallocated-share-per-month row. Sort option by cost descending (see R4) so the buckets surface when large.
- **Must not.** Never redistribute unallocated amounts to known projects and never call them overhead or bench cost (ADR-0016 at `docs/adr/0016-project-employee-cost-from-recorded-payroll.md:5`, glossary at `GLOSSARY.md:99-105`, spec at `docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-spec.md:55`).

### R4 — Concentration and largest movers in Monthly/FY

- **Problem.** Monthly/FY sort alphabetically (§1.4), so the most expensive project and the fastest riser hide. Expenditure already ranks by largest cost and largest increase (§1.2), but fallback readers and FY planners get no equivalent. The spec's owner stories explicitly want both rankings (`docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-spec.md:28-29`) and employee-level drivers (`docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-spec.md:32`).
- **Evidence already available.** The ranking contract (same figures, inside one currency, unknown unranked with reason, ties share positions at `GLOSSARY.md:155-157`) and the payload-driven ordering pattern (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/expenditure-view.tsx:1037-1070`).
- **Change.** Add cost/increase ordering toggles to Monthly By Project and By Employee; add Top-5 + "Top 3 share of month %" strip; add per-row share-of-month %; in FY, add FY-total ranking and largest FY-mover columns. All shares derive from the same filtered detail the table shows, never by re-aggregating a different scope (the filtered-subtotal principle at `src/lib/company-expenditure/types.ts:878-882`).
- **Must not.** Do not rank across currencies and do not place unknown-comparison rows by increase (`GLOSSARY.md:155-157`).

### R5 — Monthly burn vs approved budgets without inventing variance

- **Problem.** Budget variance publishes only for approved, exact-month, same-project/currency/scope budgets (§1.6). Annual or partial budgets are disclosed as incompatible and never prorated (`src/lib/company-expenditure/budget-comparison.ts:6-10`). An owner asking "are we overspending this month against the annual plan?" gets "Different period", which is correct but not actionable for monthly control.
- **Evidence already available.** Covering-budget reads, candidate flags (matches-month vs overlaps-month), and outcome vocabulary (`src/lib/company-expenditure/budget-comparison.ts:63-103`, `src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/budget-section.tsx:128-138`); period columns on the budget record (`migrations/20261008092100_project_cost_budgets.js:25-26`).
- **Change (two disciplined options, not silent proration).** (a) Support monthly-phased budgets: one approved row per month (or explicit monthly phasing on the record) so each month compares exactly, per the existing rule. (b) Until then, show an explicitly labelled run-rate reference ("Annual budget ÷ 12 = X/month, reference only — not a variance") beside the official outcome, with the variance cell still stating `incompatible_period`. Add outcome-count KPIs (compared / missing / unapproved / incompatible) so the owner sees budget coverage at a glance. Either way, remaining/overspent stays defined only against confirmed Incurred Project Cost for that month and "is not profit, recognized revenue, or a forecast" (`src/lib/company-expenditure/budget-comparison.ts:20-22`).
- **Must not.** Never allocate an annual budget proportionally into a variance, never convert currencies to force a comparison, and never read commercial value/quotes/orders as budgets (`src/lib/company-expenditure/budget-comparison.ts:6-17`, `migrations/20261008092100_project_cost_budgets.js:4-9`); never label order value minus cost as profit (`docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-spec.md:66`).

### R6 — Incurred-vs-paid bridge at month grain

- **Problem.** Monthly/FY show cost only; cash paid lives in a collapsed Expenditure section (§1.6). An owner controlling monthly expenses needs both "what did we owe for this month's work" and "what left the bank this month" without conflating them. The accounting note requires exactly this separation: "paying a supplier settles a liability; it does not create a second cost. Report cash paid as a separate column/statement" (`docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-accounting.md:25`).
- **Evidence already available.** Paid-by-currency with settlement/payroll/petty splits, funding apart from paid, per-target cover states, and legacy disclosures (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/cash-section.tsx:59-94`, `src/lib/company-expenditure/cash.ts:1-25`).
- **Change.** Add a month-grain bridge card row to Expenditure and a compact version to Monthly (for readers with cash-read access): Incurred Cost (reporting basis) · Cash Paid (same currency only) · Outstanding on month's targets · Funding outside paid. Keep each currency separate and state unconvertible months per currency, following the existing conversion discipline (`src/lib/company-expenditure/index.ts:62-67`). Link each figure to its drilldown (cost drilldown + cash targets), since the owner story wants source documents behind totals (`docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-spec.md:64`).
- **Must not.** Never add cost and cash into one total and never treat client order value as cost or revenue (`docs/adr/0017-project-incurred-cost-and-commitments-are-separate.md:3-7`, `docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-spec.md:15`).

### R7 — Close/revision stability in Monthly/FY headers

- **Problem.** Only Expenditure carries close status, frozen totals, blockers/warnings, and revision history (§1.6). A Monthly/FY reader seeing "Total Cost" cannot tell whether the month is open (still movable by recognition, accruals, payroll) or closed (immutable except by explicit revision). Payroll finalization freezes attribution but "never closes the month" (`GLOSSARY.md:266-268`).
- **Evidence already available.** The close payload (status, frozen reconciliation, review) and revision payload (history + candidates) already ride the expenditure response (`src/app/api/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/route.ts:195-223`); the review maps wired-source/coverage state to blockers vs warnings (`src/lib/company-expenditure/close.ts:231-283`).
- **Change.** Attach the same close summary to the Monthly/FY responses (status + frozen company total + blocker count) and render a header badge: Open (N blockers) / Closed (frozen X, R revisions). In Monthly, add a frozen-vs-live delta line when revisions post-date the close. Reuse the existing close/read gates — no new privilege surface (`src/app/api/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/route.ts:113-122`).
- **Must not.** Never label an incomplete month complete; blockers stay blocking and warnings stay disclosed (`src/lib/company-expenditure/close.ts:262-283`, `docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-spec.md:57-58`).

### R8 — Department, pay-stream, and client subtotals

- **Problem.** Monthly rows carry department and designation (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/data-source.ts:195-215`) and the interpretation carries pay stream per employee (`src/lib/company-expenditure/types.ts:1192-1199`), but no view subtotals by department, designation, pay stream, or client. "Which part of the company grew?" requires manual spreadsheet pivots. Both pay streams must be covered (`docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-spec.md:38`), yet their mix is invisible.
- **Change.** Add optional group-by (Department / Pay stream / Client) to Monthly By Employee and By Project with subtotal rows; add payroll-vs-contract cost/hour cards to the Monthly header; add the same grouping to the corresponding Excel sheets. Pay-stream grouping reads the frozen stream for recorded rows and the profile-derived stream for estimates, matching the loader's derivation (`src/lib/company-expenditure/payroll.ts:760-768`).
- **Must not.** Do not filter the population to the Utilization Payroll Roster (`docs/adr/0016-project-employee-cost-from-recorded-payroll.md:12`); disclose missing-slip/pricing employees inside their groups rather than dropping them (`src/lib/company-expenditure/payroll.ts:970-985`).

### R9 — Monthly/FY Excel evidence parity

- **Problem.** Monthly workbooks carry cost-only columns and FY workbooks carry hours/cost matrices, but neither carries the confidence the JSON already computes (§1.7). A downloaded "Total Cost" is therefore less trustworthy than the screen it came from, inverting the spec's export contract (§1.7). The expenditure workbook already sets the pattern: reconciliation + project detail + budgets/commitments + cash + revisions/close with a coverage table (`src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/excel-template.ts:2547-2563`, `src/app/reports/employee-project-monthly-cost/excel-template.ts:1598-1638`).
- **Change.** Add Recorded, Estimated, and Status columns to all monthly sheets; add a Coverage sheet (code/label/severity/detail) and a Close line (status/frozen total/version) to monthly and FY workbooks; fix the stale "4 sheets" header comment to the six sheets actually built (§1.7). Keep the existing tints/fonts/borders and frozen panes; add columns, not a redesign.
- **Must not.** Exports must use the same period, currency, scope, version, evidence states, and interpretation as the screen (`docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-spec.md:151`); filtered exports must keep company totals intact while showing the filtered detail, per the glossary's export definition (`GLOSSARY.md:274-276`).

### R10 — Filtered totals, footnote, and currency labels

- **Problem (three small misreads).** (a) Search narrows rows but cards and grand totals stay full-month (§1.4), so "search Engineering → Total Cost" misstates Engineering's cost. (b) The footer footnote describes the retired gross-first rate formula (§1.4) while the code prices from frozen allocations or the corrected payroll estimate (§1.3). (c) Monthly/FY money renders through the INR formatter (`src/lib/format.js:35-40`) without naming the currency, while the reconciliation deliberately states currency per figure and refuses to label unknown money as INR (§1.8).
- **Change.** (a) Add a "Filtered subtotal" line (hours/cost/employees/projects for the visible rows) above the unchanged company grand total, following the `filtered_subtotal` pattern that narrows detail without replacing the company reconciliation (`src/lib/company-expenditure/types.ts:878-882`). (b) Rewrite the footnote to the true rule: recorded frozen employer-cost allocation where finalized, corrected payroll-calculation estimate otherwise, hours from daily activity logs (cite §1.3 sources in the footnote's title attribute or a methodology popover). (c) Label every Monthly/FY money header and Excel title with its currency ("Cost (INR, payroll)") and state the payroll-currency basis (`src/lib/company-expenditure/payroll.ts:51`).
- **Must not.** None of these changes may alter a computed amount — presentation only, reusing the existing Decimal totals and formatters (`src/lib/money.ts:15-28`, `src/lib/format.js:35-75`).

- Single financial interpretation: all views read recorded allocations or the corrected estimate, never a parallel salary/currency/date calculation (§1.3), as the spec requires (`docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-spec.md:96-98`).
- Exact-cent allocation that foots to each slip, with attributable rounding (`src/lib/company-expenditure/payroll.ts:101-140`).
- Unknown-is-null discipline across amounts, currencies, priors, and cost-to-date (§1.2, §1.3), matching "a missing amount is unknown, not zero" (`docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-spec.md:103`).
- Unfinished-month comparison discipline with disclosed windows and late-entry handling (§1.2).
- Budget, commitment, cash, and client-order separation with no invented profit (`docs/adr/0017-project-incurred-cost-and-commitments-are-separate.md:3-7`, `docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-spec.md:200-207`).
- Versioned close and revision controls with frozen snapshots and journals (§1.6).

## 4. Guardrails for any implementation

- Management report, not the books: label estimates, mark coverage gaps, never present figures as audited or filed (`docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-accounting.md:9-14`); books stay on accrual double-entry by statute, and this report is not those books (`docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-accounting.md:13`).
- Recognize cost at the service period; invoice is evidence, payment settles liability; accrue month-end received-but-uninvoiced work and mark every estimate with source/owner/basis/reversal expectation (`docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-accounting.md:18-23`, `docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-accounting.md:65-72`).
- Recoverable taxes are not cost; eligibility is per-item with documents under CGST ss.16–17, never by naming convention (`docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-accounting.md:24`).
- Keep deliberate overhead distinct from unknown allocation; project overhead allocation needs an explicit approved policy before it is reported (`docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-accounting.md:43-56`, `docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-spec.md:112`).
- Commitments are executory and disclosed separately; cancellable orders create no obligation; future operating losses are never provisioned as cost (`docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-accounting.md:58-63`).
- Out of scope: general ledger, audited statements, revenue/profit engine, automatic overhead/unknown allocation, direction/currency/invoice guessing, uncommitted-work forecasts, fixed-asset register, Utilization changes, silent slip regeneration, destructive cleanup (`docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-spec.md:200-211`).
- Verification intent stands even though the Playwright harness was removed: drive the running app against a real database and verify persisted financial evidence independently (`docs/app/reports/project-expenditure-spec.md:162-170`).
